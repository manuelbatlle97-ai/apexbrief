## Texans WR Collins Sidelined from Practice

Houston Texans wide receiver Nico Collins was not present at Thursday's practice session, marking another day the offensive weapon remains sidelined with a hamstring injury. The absence continues a concerning trend for the team as they prepare for their next contest.

## Injury Update Raises Concerns

Collins' hamstring issue has become an ongoing concern for the Texans' offense. The star receiver's inability to practice throws his availability into question for upcoming games. The team has not yet provided a definitive timeline for his return to full participation.

## Impact on Houston's Offense

Collins' presence on the field has been a critical component of the Texans' passing attack this season. His absence from practice limits the team's ability to develop chemistry with quarterback and offensive coordinators during critical preparation periods. The situation underscores the importance of maintaining health among key personnel.

## What's Next for Collins

The Texans will continue to monitor Collins' condition throughout the week. Coaches and medical staff will make determinations about his availability closer to game time. The team may need to adjust offensive schemes if Collins cannot return to practice soon.

## Team Response

Houston's coaching staff has not made official statements regarding the severity of Collins' hamstring strain or expected recovery timeline. The organization typically takes a cautious approach with soft tissue injuries to prevent aggravation and long-term damage.

Fans and fantasy football participants closely watching Collins' status will await further updates from the team regarding his participation in upcoming practices and games.
