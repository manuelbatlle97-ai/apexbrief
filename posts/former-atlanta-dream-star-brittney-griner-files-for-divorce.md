## WNBA Star Files for Divorce

Former Atlanta Dream standout Brittney Griner filed for divorce from her wife, Cherelle, in Fulton County on Thursday, according to court documents. The filing represents a major personal development for the veteran basketball player who has been a prominent figure in the WNBA.

## Professional Career

Griner's career with the Atlanta Dream spanned multiple seasons, during which she established herself as one of the league's most talented and recognizable players. Her time in Atlanta contributed significantly to her prominence in professional women's basketball, where she has been known for her exceptional athleticism and on-court performance.

## Personal Life

The divorce filing comes as a notable development in Griner's personal life. The couple's relationship had been publicly documented, with Cherelle often appearing at games and events supporting the WNBA star. The specifics regarding custody, assets, or other divorce proceedings have not been detailed in initial court filings.

## Impact and Next Steps

The divorce proceeding will be handled through Fulton County courts. As with all divorce cases, the process will likely involve standard legal proceedings and determinations regarding property division and other matters as outlined by Georgia law.

Griner continues to be recognized as one of women's basketball's most accomplished athletes. Her career achievements and impact on the WNBA have made her one of the sport's most prominent figures both on and off the court.
