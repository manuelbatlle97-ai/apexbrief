## Legendary Sportscaster Dan Hicks Dies

Dan Hicks, a prominent voice in sports broadcasting for more than three decades, has died at age 64. The veteran announcer spent 35 years at NBC Sports, establishing himself as one of the most recognizable commentators in the industry.

Hicks was best known as the lead announcer for professional golf coverage, a role that made him a fixture in households across America during major tournaments. Beyond the fairways, his distinctive voice also resonated through Olympic broadcasting, where he covered swimming and skiing events for NBC's Games coverage. Additionally, Hicks served as an announcer for Notre Dame football, bringing his expertise to one of college sports' most storied programs.

## A Legendary Career in Sports Media

Throughout his career, Hicks became synonymous with quality sports journalism and commentary. His professionalism and deep knowledge of the sports he covered made him a trusted presence for millions of viewers. The combination of his work across golf, Olympic events, and collegiate football demonstrated his versatility as a broadcaster and his ability to excel across different sporting disciplines.

Hicks' impact on sports broadcasting extended beyond his on-air presence. He helped define how golf tournaments, Olympic competitions, and Notre Dame games were presented to American audiences during a transformative era in sports media. His tenure at NBC Sports spanned decades during which the network expanded its sports coverage and elevated its broadcasting standards.

## Remembering a Sports Broadcasting Icon

The news of Hicks' passing marks the loss of a respected figure in the sports media landscape. His 35-year career at NBC Sports represents a significant chapter in the network's broadcasting history. Colleagues and fans alike have remembered Hicks for his professionalism, expertise, and dedication to covering sports with integrity and enthusiasm.

Dan Hicks leaves behind a legacy as one of the most accomplished announcers in sports broadcasting history. His voice will remain part of the memories of countless sporting moments for viewers who followed his coverage of golf majors, Olympic competitions, and college football games throughout his distinguished career.
