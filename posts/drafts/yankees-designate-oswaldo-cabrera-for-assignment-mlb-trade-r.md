## Yankees Make Roster Move

The New York Yankees have designated infielder/outfielder Oswaldo Cabrera for assignment, according to MLB Trade Rumors. The move comes as part of the team's ongoing roster management as the season progresses.

## What Designation Means

When a player is designated for assignment, it means the team has given the player seven days to be claimed by another MLB club through waivers. If no team claims the player during this period, the Yankees retain the right to trade, release, or reassign him to their minor league system. This designation creates roster flexibility for the club.

## About Cabrera

Cabrera has been utilized by the Yankees as a utility player, capable of playing both infield and outfield positions. His versatility has made him a useful option for roster depth, though the designation indicates the team is exploring other options at this stage.

## Roster Implications

The designation frees up a roster spot for the Yankees, allowing the organization to call up another player or make additional moves to address their current needs. Teams often use these moves to shuffle their rosters and test the trade market, seeing which players might attract interest from other organizations.

## Looking Ahead

As the waiver period unfolds over the next week, the baseball community will monitor whether any clubs show interest in claiming Cabrera. His eventual destination—whether that's with another major league team, back to the Yankees organization, or elsewhere—remains to be determined. The move is part of the ongoing roster adjustments teams make throughout the season in pursuit of on-field success.
