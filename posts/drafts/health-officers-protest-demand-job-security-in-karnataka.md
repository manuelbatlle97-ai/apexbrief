## Thousands Demand Job Security and Better Wages

More than 5,200 community health officers in Karnataka staged a significant protest at Freedom Park, demanding immediate action on employment regularisation, pay revision, and job security. The large-scale demonstration highlighted growing frustrations among healthcare workers over their employment conditions and compensation packages.

## Key Demands of the Protesters

The protesting health officers raised multiple demands during the demonstration. Primary among these were permanent regularisation of their positions, revision of salaries to match their counterparts in other states, and assurance of long-term job security. The workers argued that their current employment status lacks stability and that their compensation does not reflect the critical nature of their work in public health delivery.

## Impact on Health Services

The protest resulted in disruption of AAM (Arogya Asha Mahila) services across Karnataka. This interruption affects health program delivery at the grassroots level, potentially impacting healthcare access for vulnerable communities that depend on these services. The disruption underscores the essential role these health officers play in Karnataka's healthcare infrastructure.

## Growing Healthcare Worker Activism

This protest reflects broader concerns among healthcare workers in India regarding employment stability and fair compensation. Community health officers are critical frontline workers responsible for disease surveillance, health awareness, immunization programs, and community engagement in their respective areas. Despite their significance, many remain on contractual or temporary positions without adequate benefits.

## Path Forward

The demonstration at Freedom Park has brought attention to systemic issues affecting Karnataka's health workforce. State health officials face mounting pressure to address the workers' grievances and find sustainable solutions for regularising their positions and improving their working conditions. The timing of the protest, coinciding with service disruptions, demonstrates the determination of health officers to secure their demands for permanent employment and wage revision before returning to normal operations.
