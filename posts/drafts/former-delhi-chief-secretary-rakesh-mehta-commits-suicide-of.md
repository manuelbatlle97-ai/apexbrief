## Former Delhi Chief Secretary Found Dead

Rakesh Mehta, a former chief secretary of Delhi who held office during Sheila Dikshit's administration, allegedly died by suicide at his Noida residence on September 6, according to officials. The death marks a significant loss within Delhi's administrative circles, where Mehta had served in a pivotal governance role.

## Investigation and Suicide Note

Police investigating the incident recovered a purported suicide note at the scene. According to officials, the note cited mental and health issues as reasons for the alleged suicide. The discovery prompted authorities to launch a formal investigation into the circumstances surrounding his death. Details from the note have not been fully disclosed to the public.

## Career and Background

Mehta was a 1975-batch Indian Administrative Service (IAS) officer who had served in various capacities throughout his distinguished career in public administration. His tenure as Delhi's chief secretary during Dikshit's governance period was marked by his involvement in significant administrative decisions. As a senior bureaucrat, he had held considerable influence over Delhi's administrative machinery.

## Mental Health Concerns

The circumstances of Mehta's death underscore ongoing concerns about mental health issues among high-ranking government officials. The pressure associated with senior administrative positions, combined with personal health challenges, can significantly impact individuals' well-being. Experts have increasingly emphasized the need for mental health support systems for government employees in demanding roles.

## Impact and Reflection

The incident has prompted reflection within Delhi's administrative community about workplace stress and mental health resources. Mehta's death adds to a broader conversation about the psychological toll of high-pressure governmental positions. Colleagues and officials have expressed condolences to his family, acknowledging his contributions to Delhi's administration over his tenure.

Authorities continue to investigate the matter while his family grieves the loss. The case underscores the importance of accessible mental health support for individuals in senior positions across government and other demanding professions.
