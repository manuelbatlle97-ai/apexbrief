## Hurts Sidelined by Calf Injury

Philadelphia Eagles quarterback Jalen Hurts will not play in Sunday's game against the Carolina Panthers after being ruled out due to a superficial blood clot in his calf, according to ESPN. The injury represents a significant blow to the Eagles' offense as they prepare to face the Panthers on the road.

## Medical Details

The blood clot, identified as superficial, has been deemed serious enough to warrant Hurts' absence from the upcoming contest. Superficial blood clots, while typically less severe than deep vein thrombosis, still require careful medical management and monitoring to prevent complications. The Eagles' medical staff made the decision to hold Hurts out as a precautionary measure.

## Impact on Eagles Offense

With Hurts unavailable, the Eagles will need to rely on their backup quarterback to lead the offense against Carolina. This development comes at a critical juncture in the NFL season, requiring the team to adjust their game plan and offensive strategy. The backup's performance could have significant implications for the team's playoff positioning and momentum.

## Timeline and Recovery

While the Eagles ruled Hurts out for this particular game, there has been no official statement regarding his expected return timeline. The team will likely provide updates on his status as the medical evaluation continues. Blood clots require proper monitoring and treatment protocols before athletes can safely return to competition.

The Eagles organization has not yet released a formal statement detailing additional medical information or addressing questions about Hurts' long-term availability.
