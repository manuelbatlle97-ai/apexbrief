The Baltimore Ravens signed quarterback Kedon Slovis from the Green Bay Packers practice squad, according to NBC Sports. The move provides additional depth at the quarterback position for the AFC North team as it navigates the remainder of the season.

## Adding to QB Depth

Slovis joins Baltimore's quarterback roster following the signing. The addition comes as the Ravens look to strengthen their bench strength behind their primary starter. Practice squad acquisitions allow teams to evaluate talent and provide insurance at critical positions heading into the final stretch of the season.

His addition to Baltimore gives him another opportunity to demonstrate his capabilities within a new system.
