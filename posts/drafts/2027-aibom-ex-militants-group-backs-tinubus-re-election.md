## Ex-Militants Forum Pledges Support for Tinubu

The Akwa Ibom Ex-Militants Forum has publicly reaffirmed its commitment to supporting President Bola Tinubu's re-election bid in 2027. The group's endorsement represents another show of political backing for the incumbent president as the nation looks ahead to the next general elections.

In their statement of support, the forum aligned itself with the Renewed Hope Agenda, which forms the cornerstone of Tinubu's administration's development and reform initiatives. The Akwa Ibom Ex-Militants Forum, which comprises former militant members from the Niger Delta region, appears confident in the direction the current administration is taking the nation.

## Backing the Renewed Hope Agenda

The forum's reaffirmation of support underscores the growing political mobilization around Tinubu's presidency heading into the 2027 election cycle. The Renewed Hope Agenda has been central to the administration's messaging, focusing on economic recovery, infrastructure development, and national development priorities.

The backing from the Akwa Ibom Ex-Militants Forum is significant given the historical importance of stakeholder support from the Niger Delta region. The group's endorsement signals continued engagement with the administration's policies and programs aimed at national development and stability.

## Political Movements Take Shape

As Nigeria's political landscape evolves ahead of 2027, various interest groups and organizations continue to declare their positions regarding the upcoming elections. The Akwa Ibom Ex-Militants Forum's public backing of Tinubu represents one of several such declarations from different sectors and regions across the country.

The forum's commitment to the Renewed Hope Agenda suggests confidence in the administration's approach to governance and national development. Their continued support provides another voice in the broader political discourse surrounding the nation's future direction and leadership priorities for the coming years.
