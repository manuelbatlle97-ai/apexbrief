## Lubbock Surgeon Inspires Future Doctors Through Hands-On Training

Dr. Thanapoom "Mo" Boonipat, a plastic surgeon and founder of FacePlus Plastic Surgery Institute in Lubbock, shared his expertise with aspiring medical professionals on October 25, 2026. The accomplished physician led an interactive suturing workshop designed specifically for women in STEM pursuing premedical degrees at Texas Tech University.

## Practical Skills Meet Career Guidance

The workshop provided students with direct instruction in fundamental surgical techniques while offering invaluable insights into the medical field. Beyond teaching hands-on skills, Dr. Boonipat conducted a career question-and-answer session, allowing participants to learn about his journey in medicine and gain perspective on various specialties within healthcare. This combination of practical training and mentorship creates a comprehensive educational experience for students navigating their medical careers.

## Supporting Women in STEM

The initiative underscores the importance of mentorship and direct engagement from medical professionals in supporting women pursuing science, technology, engineering, and mathematics fields. By hosting such workshops, accomplished physicians help bridge the gap between classroom learning and real-world medical practice, while also demonstrating viable career pathways for underrepresented groups in medicine.

## Building the Next Generation

Dr. Boonipat's commitment to educating the next generation of physicians reflects a broader trend among medical professionals to invest in community outreach and student development. Events like this suturing workshop provide students with competitive advantages as they prepare for medical school applications and future careers in healthcare.

The workshop demonstrates how experienced professionals can meaningfully contribute to their communities while fostering interest in medicine among qualified students. For Texas Tech's premed students, the opportunity to learn from an established plastic surgeon in their own city provides both educational value and professional inspiration.
