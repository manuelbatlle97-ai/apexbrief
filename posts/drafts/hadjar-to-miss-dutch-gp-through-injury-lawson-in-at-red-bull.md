## Hadjar Sidelined by Wrist Injury

Isack Hadjar will not compete at this weekend's Dutch Grand Prix after sustaining a wrist injury during the summer shutdown. The injury forced Red Bull Racing to make a last-minute driver change for the Zandvoort circuit, one of the most demanding races on the Formula 1 calendar.

## Lawson Confirmed as Replacement

Red Bull has officially announced Liam Lawson as Hadjar's replacement for the Dutch GP. Lawson's appointment marks another opportunity for the talented driver to gain experience in a top-tier team during a critical period of the season.

## Timing of the Injury

The wrist injury occurred during the summer break, giving Hadjar insufficient time to recover before the high-speed challenges of Zandvoort. The Dutch circuit's demanding layout requires drivers to be in peak physical condition, making the decision to sideline Hadjar a prudent one for both driver safety and team performance.

## Impact on Red Bull's Campaign

The driver change comes at a significant point in the F1 season, requiring Red Bull to adapt its strategy and preparations. Lawson's inclusion provides the team with a capable alternative while Hadjar recovers from his injury.

## Looking Ahead

Hadjar's status for subsequent races remains to be determined as the team monitors his recovery. Lawson will look to make the most of his opportunity at one of Formula 1's most iconic venues.
