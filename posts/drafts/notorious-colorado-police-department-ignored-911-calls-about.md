## Community Garden Destroyed After Police Inaction

A Colorado police department faces scrutiny after allegedly ignoring 911 calls about a man vandalizing a community garden on E 16th Avenue in Aurora on August 8. According to reports, Worku Woldemariam, 42, ripped apart the urban garden and damaged bee hives while multiple callers tried to alert authorities to the ongoing destruction.

## Swift Response When Suspect Collapsed

The response timeline drew criticism when officers rapidly arrived at the scene after Woldemariam collapsed, providing medical assistance to the same man whose destructive actions they had reportedly failed to address through earlier emergency calls. The contrast between the delayed response to property destruction and the immediate assistance provided to the suspect has raised questions about police department priorities and 911 call handling procedures.

## Pattern of Alleged Vandalism

Woldemariam was accused of involvement in several other vandalism cases prior to the August 8 incident at the community garden. The repeated allegations suggest a pattern of concerning behavior in the Aurora area, with community members apparently attempting to alert authorities through the emergency response system.

## Questions About Police Department Response

The incident highlights ongoing concerns about response protocols in the Aurora Police Department, which has faced scrutiny in recent years over various operational issues. The significant difference in response times—from unresponded 911 calls about active property destruction to immediate arrival after a medical emergency—underscores potential gaps in how departments prioritize and dispatch resources.

## Impact on Community Trust

The community garden represents local efforts to build green spaces and provide resources to residents. The destruction of such community assets, combined with the apparent police inaction, may affect public confidence in emergency services and neighborhood safety perceptions among Aurora residents who depend on 911 services to respond to incidents.

Details regarding the outcome of the incident and any potential charges against Woldemariam have not been fully disclosed. The Aurora Police Department's statement on the matter, if available, was not included in initial reports.
