As with most securities class actions, there are critical deadlines that shareholders must meet to preserve their legal rights. The firm has emphasized the importance of timely action, as missing these deadlines could result in investors losing their opportunity to participate in any potential settlement or judgment. Eligible shareholders should review their holdings and transaction records during the relevant period.

## About Securities Class Actions

Securities class actions allow groups of investors who have suffered losses to collectively pursue legal claims against companies and their officers or directors. These lawsuits typically allege violations of federal securities laws, including allegations of misrepresentation or omission of material information that affected stock prices.
