## Nikki Bella Takes Time Out From Dating

Nikki Bella is stepping back from her romantic pursuits, telling TMZ that she's embraced celibacy as she focuses on her hectic work schedule. The former WWE superstar made light of her decision, joking that she has become "like a nun" following her recent relationship with Cooper DeJean.

## Finding Balance in Busy Schedule

Bella's announcement reflects a conscious choice to prioritize her professional commitments over dating. With multiple projects keeping her occupied, the star has decided that taking time away from intimate relationships is the best approach for her current lifestyle. She presented the decision with humor, suggesting that her celibate status allows her to concentrate fully on the demands of her career without the complications that come with romantic involvement.

## Recent Romance With DeJean

The timing of Bella's celibacy reveal comes after her romantic involvement with Cooper DeJean made headlines. While details about the nature and duration of their relationship remain limited, the announcement suggests that Bella is using this period to reset and refocus on herself. Her decision to openly discuss her celibate status demonstrates her willingness to be candid about her personal life with the media.

## Life Beyond the Ring

Nikki Bella has maintained a high-profile career both during and after her time in professional wrestling. Beyond her WWE legacy, she has pursued various entertainment ventures, including appearances on reality television and other entertainment projects. Her busy schedule underscores the challenges that come with maintaining a public profile while managing personal relationships.

## Moving Forward

Bella's decision to embrace celibacy, at least temporarily, appears to be a healthy boundary she's setting for herself during a demanding period in her career. By being open about her choice, she continues to engage authentically with her fanbase while maintaining control over her personal narrative. Whether this period of celibacy remains temporary or evolves into a longer lifestyle choice, Bella's focus on her professional goals suggests her priorities are clearly defined as she navigates her post-wrestling career.
