## Ongoing Search Operations in Kulgam

Security forces continued large-scale cordon and search operations Friday in Kulgam as they hunt for terrorists responsible for opening fire on Central Reserve Police Force (CRPF) personnel. The injured jawans were receiving medical attention following the attack, prompting authorities to intensify their search efforts across the region.

## Response to Armed Militant Attack

The shooting incident triggered an immediate security response with multiple agencies coordinating the search operation. Forces established cordons in and around the Kulgam area to prevent the escape of suspected militants. The operation represented a significant deployment of personnel dedicated to locating those responsible for the attack on the CRPF team.

## Security Situation in Kashmir Valley

The attack underscores ongoing security challenges in the Kashmir Valley, where militant groups continue to pose threats to security personnel and civilians. Kulgam district has witnessed periodic militant activity, making the region a focus of counter-terrorism operations. Security forces maintain regular patrols and conduct search operations to disrupt militant networks operating in the area.

## Coordinated Law Enforcement Efforts

The search operation involved coordination between multiple security agencies working to gather intelligence and track suspected militant movements. Forces utilized information from ground sources and surveillance to narrow the search perimeter. The operation continued beyond initial contact, reflecting the determination to apprehend those responsible for the attack.

## Impact on Ground Security

Incidents like the CRPF shooting highlight the persistent security situation requiring constant vigilance from law enforcement. Security forces remain committed to protecting personnel and civilians while pursuing active threats in the region. The ongoing search operations are part of broader counter-terrorism efforts aimed at maintaining stability in the Kashmir Valley and eliminating militant threats.

Authorities have not yet released detailed casualty figures or information regarding the condition of the injured personnel. Updates on the search operation's progress are expected to emerge as security forces continue their efforts to locate the suspects responsible for the attack.
