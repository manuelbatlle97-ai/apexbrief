## Welsh Boxer Eccles Calls Time on Career

Welsh boxer Eccles has announced her retirement from professional boxing, bringing an end to her time in the sport. The decision marks the conclusion of a career that saw the fighter compete at the highest levels of amateur and professional boxing.

## Emotional Farewell

In confirming her retirement, Eccles used the phrase "full heart, empty tank" to describe her current state, suggesting emotional fulfillment from her career achievements but physical and mental exhaustion from the demands of professional competition. The fighter expressed that she has given everything she could to the sport.

## Critical Comments on Career Obstacles

Eccles did not mince words when discussing those who attempted to undermine her career. She launched a verbal attack on what she termed "awful" people who tried to "end" her boxing journey. The comments suggest the athlete faced significant challenges and potentially hostile treatment from individuals within or around the sport during her career.

## Reflection on Career Impact

While specific details of her professional record and major achievements were not detailed, Eccles' decision to retire and her willingness to speak openly about the obstacles she faced highlight the challenges athletes can encounter beyond competition itself. Her retirement statement serves as a reflection on both the physical and emotional toll of professional boxing.

## Moving Forward

With her retirement now official, Eccles' legacy in Welsh boxing will remain a topic of discussion among fans and analysts of the sport. The fighter's candid remarks about those who sought to harm her career underscore broader issues within professional athletics regarding athlete treatment and support systems. Her departure from the ring represents the end of one chapter, though her impact on Welsh boxing history is already established.
