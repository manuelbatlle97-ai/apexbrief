## Russian Officials Report No Plague Among Lab Worker Contacts

Russian health authorities said Tuesday that plague has not been detected among close contacts of a worker at an anti-plague laboratory in Siberia who died last week. The announcement comes as officials continue testing individuals who had direct contact with the deceased laboratory employee.

## Testing Underway in Siberian Lab Investigation

According to Russian officials, approximately 60% of the identified close contacts have been tested so far, with no positive results for plague detected. The worker's death prompted immediate health investigations and contact tracing efforts to determine if the disease had spread beyond the initial case. Health authorities have prioritized testing of those who had direct exposure to the deceased individual.

## Public Health Response Continues

The incident has highlighted the ongoing work of Russia's anti-plague laboratories, which maintain disease surveillance and research operations across the country's vast Siberian regions. These specialized facilities play a critical role in monitoring and preventing outbreaks of infectious diseases. The death of the laboratory worker has prompted enhanced safety protocols and comprehensive contact investigation procedures.

## Containment Measures in Effect

While no additional plague cases have been confirmed among contacts tested to date, Russian health officials continue their investigation. The remaining 40% of identified close contacts are expected to complete testing in the coming days. Officials have not disclosed specific details about the laboratory worker's death or the circumstances surrounding the exposure.

## Ongoing Monitoring

The situation demonstrates the potential risks associated with work in high-containment research facilities, even when proper safety protocols are in place. Russia's health system continues to monitor the situation closely to ensure public safety. As testing progresses among the remaining close contacts, authorities will provide updates on any additional findings. The laboratory has reportedly maintained strict safety standards, though the exact cause of the worker's death remains under investigation.
