## New Jersey Celebrates Class of 2026 at Hall of Fame Induction

More than 1,000 New Jerseyans came together at the Dream Live Performing Arts Center in East Rutherford last night to commemorate the 18th Annual New Jersey Hall of Fame (NJHOF) Induction Ceremony. The event recognized 16 distinguished individuals who have made significant impacts on the state and beyond through their accomplishments in various fields.

## A Tradition of Recognition

The New Jersey Hall of Fame ceremony represents one of the state's premier events celebrating excellence and achievement. Now in its 18th year, the induction ceremony has become a hallmark tradition for honoring Garden State icons who have contributed to the cultural, social, and professional landscape of New Jersey and the nation.

## Class of 2026 Joins Prestigious Legacy

The ceremony marks the addition of 16 new members to the NJHOF, joining hundreds of previously inducted honorees. These individuals represent the diverse talents and accomplishments that have helped define New Jersey's identity as a state known for producing exceptional leaders, innovators, and contributors across multiple disciplines.

## Community Gathering and Celebration

The packed venue at Dream Live Performing Arts Center demonstrated the strong community support for recognizing outstanding New Jerseyans. The gathering underscored the importance the state places on honoring individuals whose dedication and achievements have elevated New Jersey's reputation both nationally and internationally.

The New Jersey Hall of Fame continues to serve as a platform for celebrating the state's rich heritage of excellence and inspiring future generations to pursue their own paths of distinction and service.
