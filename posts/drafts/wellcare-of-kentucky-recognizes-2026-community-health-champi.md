## WellCare of Kentucky Celebrates Community Health Leadership

WellCare of Kentucky recognized exceptional community health advocates at its seventh annual Community Health Champions Awards ceremony held at Churchill Downs in Louisville. Twelve individuals and organizations were honored for their outstanding contributions to improving health and wellness in Kentucky communities.

## Recognition and Support for Local Champions

The award recipients represent a diverse group of health advocates and organizations dedicated to addressing critical health challenges across the state. By recognizing these champions, WellCare of Kentucky highlighted the vital role community leaders play in promoting public health and expanding access to care. The awards ceremony brought together healthcare professionals, community members, and organizational leaders to celebrate these achievements.

## Strengthening Community Health Initiatives

Each award recipient received recognition alongside grants to support their organizations' ongoing health initiatives. These grants demonstrate WellCare of Kentucky's commitment to investing in grassroots health programs and community-based solutions. The funding enables recipients to expand their reach and deepen their impact on the communities they serve.

## Supporting Health Equity Across Kentucky

The Community Health Champions Awards program reflects WellCare of Kentucky's broader mission to improve health outcomes and address health disparities. By amplifying the work of community leaders, the organization reinforces the importance of collaborative approaches to healthcare. The awards recognize efforts across various health focus areas, from disease prevention to health education and access initiatives.

The seventh annual ceremony underscores WellCare of Kentucky's dedication to partnering with local organizations and individuals working on the front lines of community health. Through recognition, funding, and ongoing collaboration, the initiative strengthens the ecosystem of health advocates working to improve wellness across Kentucky.
