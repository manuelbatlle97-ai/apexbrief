## Major Systems Overhaul Underway at IAG

International Airlines Group is undertaking a significant SAP migration project that extends beyond routine software upgrades. The initiative represents a comprehensive modernization of the airline group's finance function, with implications that reach far beyond traditional enterprise resource planning implementation.

## Building Blocks for Artificial Intelligence

The SAP migration serves as a critical foundation for introducing artificial intelligence capabilities into IAG's finance operations. By standardizing processes and improving data quality through the new system, the organization is creating the infrastructure necessary for AI applications to function effectively. This approach demonstrates a strategic understanding that AI initiatives depend heavily on clean, well-organized data and streamlined business processes.

## Process and Data Refinement

The migration has brought heightened attention to how IAG's finance function operates at a fundamental level. Implementation teams have been forced to examine existing processes, identify redundancies, and establish data standards that serve both current operations and future technological advancement. This comprehensive review ensures that legacy practices and data inconsistencies don't transfer into the new system, which could undermine both operational efficiency and AI initiatives.

The focus on data quality is particularly important for a large airline group managing complex financial operations across multiple entities and regions. Accurate, consistent financial data is essential for decision-making and regulatory compliance, and becomes even more critical when AI systems rely on historical data to make recommendations or identify patterns.

## Strategic Positioning

By integrating SAP implementation with AI readiness, IAG is positioning itself for competitive advantage in an industry increasingly dependent on data-driven insights. The airline sector faces pressure to optimize operations, manage costs, and improve customer experiences—all areas where AI can provide significant value when built on solid data foundations.

The migration represents a multi-year commitment that will reshape how IAG approaches financial management and data governance. As the implementation progresses, the organization will likely discover additional opportunities to enhance automation and analytical capabilities within its finance function, creating a more agile and responsive finance organization capable of supporting broader business objectives.
