## El-Sayed Campaign Reports Strong Fundraising Amid Stevens Endorsement

Abdul El-Sayed's campaign announced a $2.4 million fundraising day as Rep. Haley Stevens (D-Michigan) made the unexpected decision to endorse Republican Mike Rogers in the closely watched Michigan race. The endorsement immediately became a flashpoint within the Democratic Party, drawing sharp condemnation from party leaders across the nation.

## Stevens' Endorsement Sparks Democratic Backlash

Stevens' decision to back Rogers instead of supporting El-Sayed, the Democratic nominee, stunned many observers and triggered swift reactions from Democratic leadership. The move represented a significant break from traditional party unity efforts and prompted denunciations from prominent figures within Democratic circles. The endorsement raised questions about party cohesion heading into the general election season.

## Campaign Momentum and Fundraising Surge

Despite the controversial endorsement, El-Sayed's campaign capitalized on the moment with its impressive $2.4 million fundraising day. The strong financial performance suggested robust grassroots support and donor enthusiasm for the Democratic nominee. The campaign's ability to mobilize resources highlighted the competitive nature of the Michigan race and the stakes both parties see in the contest.

## Election Implications

The dynamics unfolding in Michigan reflect broader tensions within the Democratic Party and the competitive landscape of 2024 elections. Stevens' endorsement of the Republican rival complicated the narrative for Democrats hoping to maintain a unified front against Republican challenges. The incident underscored the intense political divisions that can emerge even within the same party at the state level.

The Michigan race continues to develop as a significant battleground, with both parties investing considerable resources and attention. El-Sayed's campaign demonstrated its capacity to generate enthusiasm and financial support from donors, suggesting the race would remain competitive through the general election phase. The endorsement controversy added an unexpected layer to what was already a closely monitored contest with national implications.
