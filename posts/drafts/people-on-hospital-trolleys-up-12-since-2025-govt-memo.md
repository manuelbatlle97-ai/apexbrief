## Hospital Trolley Crisis Worsens

The number of patients occupying hospital trolleys during morning counts has climbed by at least 12% since 2025, according to a confidential memo presented to Cabinet. The figures underscore mounting pressure on the nation's healthcare infrastructure and raise fresh concerns about emergency department overcrowding.

## Government Acknowledges Rising Numbers

The increase was disclosed in official Cabinet documentation, indicating that senior government officials are now formally addressing the trolley crisis. Morning counts, which capture patient numbers waiting on trolleys in hospital corridors and emergency departments, serve as a key indicator of healthcare system strain. The double-digit surge signals deteriorating conditions for patients awaiting beds and treatment.

## What the Numbers Mean

Morning trolley counts have long been used to measure healthcare system capacity. When counts rise substantially, it typically reflects a combination of factors including bed shortages, delayed patient discharges, and increased emergency department admissions. The 12% increase represents a measurable worsening of a problem that has troubled the healthcare system for years.

## Broader Healthcare Concerns

The trolley surge comes amid ongoing debates about hospital funding, staffing levels, and bed capacity. Patient advocacy groups have repeatedly warned that trolley waits compromise care quality and patient dignity. The latest figures provide quantifiable evidence of these concerns, putting specific numbers to complaints heard from patients and healthcare workers.

## Looking Forward

With Cabinet now formally briefed on the escalating trolley situation, pressure may mount for concrete action and policy responses. Healthcare officials will likely face questions about plans to address the surge, including potential investments in additional beds, improved discharge processes, and emergency department efficiency measures. The memo's disclosure suggests the government acknowledges the scale of the challenge facing the healthcare system.
