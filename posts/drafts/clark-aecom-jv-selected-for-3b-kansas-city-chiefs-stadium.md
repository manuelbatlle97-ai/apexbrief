## Clark and AECOM Selected for Major Chiefs Stadium Project

A joint venture between construction giant Clark and engineering firm AECOM has been selected to develop a new 70,000-seat stadium for the Kansas City Chiefs. The project represents a significant infrastructure investment for the region and is part of a broader economic development strategy in Kansas.

## Project Scope and Investment Details

The stadium construction is anchored within a larger $4.5 billion capital investment plan that extends beyond the venue itself. In addition to the main stadium, the comprehensive plan includes a $300 million practice facility designed to serve the team's operational needs. The broader development area also encompasses $1.2 billion in adjacent projects, creating a multi-faceted sports and entertainment complex.

## Economic Impact and Regional Development

The selection of the Clark and AECOM joint venture underscores confidence in the partnership's ability to execute a project of this magnitude. The combined expertise of both firms positions them to manage the complex logistics, design requirements, and construction challenges associated with a modern NFL stadium. The total investment reflects Kansas's commitment to attracting and retaining a major professional sports franchise.

## Building for the Future

The 70,000-seat capacity indicates the stadium is designed to accommodate the Kansas City Chiefs' fan base while providing modern amenities and infrastructure expected in contemporary sports venues. The inclusion of a dedicated practice facility within the same development area represents an integrated approach to team operations and fan experience.

This project represents one of the largest sports infrastructure investments in recent memory, demonstrating the significant capital deployment required to build world-class professional sports facilities in the current market. The partnership between Clark and AECOM brings together construction management expertise with engineering capabilities essential for a project of this scale and complexity.

The development is expected to generate economic benefits through job creation during construction and ongoing operations, while also enhancing the region's sports and entertainment offerings.
