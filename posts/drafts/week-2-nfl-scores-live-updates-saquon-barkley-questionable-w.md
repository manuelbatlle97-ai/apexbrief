Saquon Barkley, one of the NFL's premier running backs, suffered a stinger during Week 2 action. The injury has left his status questionable moving forward, raising concerns for Eagles fans and fantasy football managers alike. Stingers, which affect the nerves in the shoulder and neck area, can vary in severity and recovery time. The Eagles will need to monitor Barkley's condition closely as they prepare for upcoming contests.
