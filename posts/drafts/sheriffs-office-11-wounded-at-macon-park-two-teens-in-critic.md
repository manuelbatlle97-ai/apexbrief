## Multiple Injuries Reported in Early Morning Incident

Eleventh people were wounded in a shooting at a Macon-area park early Sunday morning, according to the Bibb County Sheriff's Office. The incident prompted an immediate law enforcement response and investigation into the circumstances surrounding the attack.

## Two Victims in Critical Condition

Among those injured, two teenagers were reported to be in critical condition following the shooting. The severity of their injuries underscored the serious nature of the incident. Additional details regarding the ages and identities of other victims were not immediately disclosed by authorities.

## Sheriff Addresses Media

Bibb County Sheriff David Davis held a media briefing Sunday afternoon to provide updates on the ongoing investigation. During the statement, Davis discussed the incident and law enforcement's response to the scene. The sheriff's office began their investigation into the circumstances that led to the shooting and the individuals responsible.

## Investigation Underway

Authorities did not immediately release information regarding a suspect or motive for the shooting. The investigation remained active as law enforcement gathered evidence and interviewed witnesses at the scene. Sheriff Davis's office asked anyone with information about the incident to come forward to assist in the ongoing investigation.

The shooting marked another incident of gun violence in the Macon area. Bibb County Sheriff's Office continued working to piece together details of what occurred during the early morning hours and to ensure public safety as the investigation progressed.
