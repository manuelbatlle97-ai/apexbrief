## Major Storm Impacts East Coast on Thanksgiving

The East Coast is experiencing severe weather conditions as a powerful storm system moves through the region on Thanksgiving Day. The system has prompted high-wind and flood warnings spanning from Virginia Beach to New York City, affecting millions of residents during one of the busiest travel days of the year.

## Transportation Disrupted Across Region

The storm is causing significant disruptions to air travel, with numerous flight delays reported at major airports throughout the affected areas. Thanksgiving is traditionally one of the busiest travel periods in the United States, and the severe weather is exacerbating travel challenges for those attempting to reach holiday destinations or return home.

## Warnings Issued From Virginia to New York

Meteorological officials have issued comprehensive high-wind and flood warnings spanning the entire corridor from Virginia Beach through New York City. These warnings indicate dangerous conditions that pose risks to residents and travelers in the region. The storm system represents the worst Thanksgiving Day weather event to impact the East Coast in recent decades, according to weather assessments.

## Widespread Power Outages and Infrastructure Concerns

Beyond flight disruptions and flooding threats, the storm is also causing widespread power outages across the region. High winds are bringing down trees and power lines, leaving thousands without electricity. These outages compound the challenges faced by residents during the holiday period, affecting heating, communication, and daily activities.

## Preparedness and Safety Measures

Authorities across the affected states are urging residents to remain alert and take necessary precautions. Those in flood-prone areas are being advised to prepare for potential evacuations, while residents throughout the region are warned to secure loose outdoor items that could become hazardous in high winds. Emergency management agencies have activated response protocols to address the anticipated impacts from the storm system.

The combination of flight delays, flooding risks, and power outages creates a challenging situation for the East Coast during the holiday weekend, with conditions expected to persist as the storm system continues its movement across the region.
