## LSU Drops from Top 10 in Coaches Poll

LSU experienced a significant fall in the latest Coaches Poll following its Saturday loss to Ole Miss in Week 4 of the college football season. The Tigers, coached by Lane Kiffin, dropped out of the top 10 for the first time after the defeat, signaling a shift in the competitive landscape of the SEC.

The loss to the Rebels marked a turning point for the LSU program, which had positioned itself as a playoff contender heading into conference play. The defeat exposed vulnerabilities in the Tigers' defense and depth, leading pollsters to reassess their championship prospects. Kiffin's squad now faces increased pressure as they continue their SEC schedule and attempt to rebuild momentum.

## Florida Gators Return to Top 25

In a contrasting storyline, the Florida Gators re-entered the Coaches Poll after maintaining an undefeated record through four games. The 3-0 Gators have impressed voters with their consistent performances early in the season, earning their spot back in the nation's top 25 rankings.

Florida's return to the poll represents a successful start to their campaign and demonstrates their potential as a conference competitor. The Gators' ability to secure victories early in the schedule has proven convincing to coaching voters, who factor team performance heavily in their balloting decisions.

## Week 4 Poll Movements

The Coaches Poll adjustments in Week 4 reflect the volatile nature of college football rankings early in the season. Teams continue to prove themselves on the field, and the poll serves as a dynamic measure of national perception. LSU's fall and Florida's re-entry exemplify how quickly fortunes can change in the competitive college football landscape.

Both SEC programs will look ahead to their next matchups with championship aspirations on the line. For LSU, the challenge involves regrouping and addressing the issues exposed in the Ole Miss game. For Florida, maintaining momentum and extending their winning streak will be critical to solidifying their position in future polls.
