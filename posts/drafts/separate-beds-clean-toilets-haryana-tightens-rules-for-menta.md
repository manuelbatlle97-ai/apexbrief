## Haryana Tightens Mental Health Facility Standards

Haryana has officially notified the Mental Healthcare Regulations 2026, introducing comprehensive guidelines designed to improve patient care and facility standards across mental health units in the state. The new regulations mandate mandatory registration for all mental health facilities and establish a registration fee of ₹25,000, setting a baseline for compliance and accountability.

## Minimum Infrastructure Requirements

The regulations specify strict minimum standards for mental health units, including provisions for separate beds for patients and clean sanitation facilities, including toilets. These infrastructure requirements aim to ensure dignified and hygienic conditions for individuals receiving mental health treatment. The focus on basic amenities reflects growing emphasis on patient dignity and recovery-conducive environments in mental healthcare facilities.

## Restraint and Patient Safety Measures

Under the new rules, mental health units face strict limitations on the use of physical or chemical restraints on patients. These restrictions are designed to protect patient rights and promote therapeutic approaches that prioritize human dignity. The regulations represent a shift toward evidence-based treatment methods that minimize coercive practices in psychiatric care.

## De-addiction Service Tracking System

A significant component of the 2026 regulations involves the implementation of a de-addiction portal that tracks services and patient progress across facilities. This centralized tracking system aims to improve accountability, ensure consistent quality of care, and provide data-driven insights into treatment outcomes across Haryana's de-addiction services.

## Implementation and Compliance

The notification of these regulations marks an important step in standardizing mental healthcare delivery across the state. By establishing clear registration requirements, facility standards, and service tracking mechanisms, Haryana aims to enhance transparency and accountability in the mental health sector. The regulations address both infrastructure concerns and treatment practices, signaling a comprehensive approach to improving mental health services for residents.
