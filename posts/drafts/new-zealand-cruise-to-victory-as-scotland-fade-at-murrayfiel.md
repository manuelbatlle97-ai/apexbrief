## All Blacks Show Second-Half Dominance

New Zealand delivered a commanding performance in the second half to defeat Scotland 43-21 at Murrayfield in the Nations Championship. The All Blacks proved too strong for their Scottish hosts, who struggled to maintain the intensity of an engaging first half.

The match showcased contrasting narratives across two halves, with Scotland mounting a competitive challenge early before New Zealand's quality and experience took over. The hosts entered halftime with genuine momentum, but the All Blacks' superior execution and tactical adjustments in the second period proved decisive.

## Scotland's First-Half Promise Fades

Scotland's competitive display in the opening 40 minutes suggested the fixture might remain closely contested throughout. However, the second half told a different story as New Zealand asserted dominance and capitalized on scoring opportunities. The All Blacks' experience in managing games at this level became evident as they controlled proceedings and gradually extended their lead.

The 22-point margin of victory ultimately reflected the difference between the two teams' performances across the full 80 minutes. While Scotland produced moments of quality rugby during the first half, consistency proved elusive against one of rugby union's premier nations.

## Nations Championship Action Continues

The match represented significant Nations Championship action, with New Zealand adding an impressive victory to their campaign. The All Blacks' ability to shift gears in the second half demonstrated their capacity to dominate international opposition when it matters most.

For Scotland, the loss highlighted the challenge of maintaining intensity against elite opponents over a full match. The team's strong opening 40 minutes provided encouragement but also frustration as they were unable to sustain that level throughout the contest at their home ground.
