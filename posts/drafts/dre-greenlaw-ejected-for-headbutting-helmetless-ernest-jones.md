## Greenlaw Ejected in Heated Moment

San Francisco 49ers linebacker Dre Greenlaw was ejected from Sunday's game against the Seattle Seahawks after headbutting opponent Ernest Jones while both players were without helmets. The incident occurred during an intense moment on the field, resulting in an immediate ejection for unsportsmanlike conduct.

## 49ers Maintain Lead Despite Personnel Loss

At the time of Greenlaw's ejection, the 49ers held a 14-10 advantage over the Seahawks. The team will now need to navigate the remainder of the contest without one of their key defensive players. Greenlaw's absence could significantly impact San Francisco's defensive schemes, particularly in coverage and run-stopping responsibilities.

## Consequences of Emotional Play

The ejection marks a significant setback for the 49ers' defensive unit. Greenlaw, a starting linebacker, provides critical contributions to the team's defensive formation. His removal forces the defensive coordinator to adjust coverage assignments and potentially rely on backup linebackers to fill the void for the remainder of the game.

## Looking Ahead

The incident highlights the challenges players face in maintaining composure during competitive games. Greenlaw will face potential fines or suspension review from the NFL for the unsportsmanlike conduct. For the 49ers, the focus now shifts to executing their game plan with available personnel and protecting their lead against the Seahawks' offensive unit.

The game continues to be closely contested as San Francisco attempts to secure the victory without their ejected linebacker leading the defense from the field.
