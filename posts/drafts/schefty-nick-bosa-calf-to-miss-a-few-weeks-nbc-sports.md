## 49ers Face Defensive Depth Challenge

San Francisco 49ers defensive end Nick Bosa will be sidelined for "a few weeks" after pulling his calf during Thursday's practice, according to ESPN's Adam Schefter. The injury represents a significant blow to the 49ers' defense as they navigate the remainder of their season.

Bosa, one of the NFL's premier pass rushers, was injured during a routine practice session. The specific circumstances surrounding the calf pull remain limited, but the timeline for his return suggests the team is taking a cautious approach to manage the injury.

## Impact on 49ers Defense

The absence of Bosa, a critical component of San Francisco's defensive line, forces the team to adjust its defensive schemes and rely on backup options. The 49ers will need to redistribute pass-rushing responsibilities among their remaining edge rushers while Bosa recovers.

This marks another challenge for a 49ers roster that has dealt with various injury concerns throughout the season. The team's depth at the defensive end position will be tested as they work to maintain their competitive edge without one of their most disruptive defenders.

## Timeline for Return

While Schefter's report indicates Bosa will miss "a few weeks," the exact timeframe for his potential return remains unclear. The 49ers may provide more specific updates as they assess the severity of the calf injury in the coming days.

Bosa's injury comes at a critical juncture in the NFL season, and the 49ers will look to maintain their performance defensively until their star pass rusher can return to full capacity. The team's medical staff will likely work closely with Bosa to ensure his calf injury heals properly before his return to game action.
