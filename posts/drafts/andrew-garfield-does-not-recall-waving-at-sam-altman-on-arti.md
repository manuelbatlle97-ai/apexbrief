## Garfield Questions Altman's Account

Actor Andrew Garfield has pushed back on OpenAI CEO Sam Altman's claim that the two had a brief interaction during filming of the upcoming biographical film "Artificial." In an interview, Garfield expressed confusion about Altman's version of events, stating he does not recall waving at the tech executive while the film was being shot.

## Altman's Alleged Sighting

According to reports, Altman claimed he received a sneak peek of Garfield's performance and said he had waved at the actor during production. The film, directed by acclaimed filmmaker Luca Guadagnino, was shot outside of Altman's residence in San Francisco. Altman's account suggested a casual encounter between the two during the shooting process.

## Garfield's Response

When asked about the interaction, Garfield responded with apparent bewilderment. "Very confused by that," the actor said regarding Altman's recollection of waving at him on set. Garfield's denial raises questions about whether the encounter actually took place or if there may have been miscommunication about the details.

## About the Upcoming Film

"Artificial" marks a significant project bringing the story of Sam Altman and OpenAI to the screen. Guadagnino, known for his meticulous directorial approach, has helmed the production which aims to explore the OpenAI CEO's rise and influence in the artificial intelligence sector. The film represents one of the first major cinematic attempts to dramatize the rapidly evolving AI industry and its key figures.

## Ongoing Promotion

As the film prepares for release, both Garfield and those involved with the production have been conducting promotional activities. The discrepancy between Altman's and Garfield's accounts has added an unexpected layer of intrigue to the film's publicity. Whether the interaction occurred or not, the exchange has generated conversation around the project and heightened public interest in seeing Garfield's portrayal of the influential tech figure.
