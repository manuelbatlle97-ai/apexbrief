## Karnataka Advances Agricultural and Tech Innovation

The Karnataka Cabinet has approved three major initiatives aimed at enhancing agricultural productivity and technological development in the state. The approvals include the Negila Yogi scheme for farmer support, a dedicated drone testing facility, and an artificial intelligence hub positioned to drive innovation across multiple sectors.

## Negila Yogi Scheme Benefits Small Farmers

The Negila Yogi scheme represents a significant step toward supporting Karnataka's agricultural community. The program will provide free cultivation services to farmers who own up to three acres of land. This targeted initiative addresses the needs of small and marginal farmers who often face financial constraints in accessing modern farming services and equipment. By removing barriers to advanced cultivation practices, the scheme aims to increase crop productivity and improve farmer incomes across the state.

## Drone Testing Facility in Chikkaballapur

The Cabinet's approval of a drone testing facility in Chikkaballapur marks Karnataka's commitment to emerging aerospace technologies. The facility will serve as a hub for advanced drone testing and development, supporting the growing unmanned aerial vehicle industry. This infrastructure is expected to attract technology companies and research institutions, positioning Chikkaballapur as a center for drone innovation. The facility will facilitate research, development, and commercialization of drone technology across various applications including agriculture, logistics, and surveillance.

## AI Hub at IIIT-B for Training and Development

The establishment of an Artificial Intelligence Hub at the Indian Institute of Information Technology-Bangalore (IIIT-B) will focus on training and skill development in AI technologies. This strategic placement at a premier technology institute ensures that the hub will benefit from existing expertise and resources. The AI hub is expected to nurture talent in artificial intelligence and machine learning, preparing professionals for emerging career opportunities in the tech sector. The initiative reflects Karnataka's positioning as a technology leader and its focus on developing human capital in cutting-edge fields.

## Strategic Development for Karnataka

These Cabinet approvals underscore Karnataka's multi-pronged approach to economic development. By simultaneously supporting agriculture through the Negila Yogi scheme while advancing technology sectors like drone development and artificial intelligence, the state is addressing diverse economic needs. The initiatives are designed to create employment opportunities, attract investment, and establish Karnataka as a hub for both agricultural innovation and advanced technology development.
