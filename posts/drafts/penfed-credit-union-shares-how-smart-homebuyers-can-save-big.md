## PenFed Credit Union Launches Educational Open House Weekend

PenFed Credit Union announced plans for a National Open House Weekend scheduled for October 23-25, designed to help prospective homebuyers understand how to save substantial amounts on closing costs. The initiative emphasizes the importance of starting the home search with a lender rather than waiting until after finding a property.

## Starting With Your Lender Makes Financial Sense

According to the credit union's guidance, homebuyers who connect with their lender early in the process can unlock significant savings opportunities. The National Open House Weekend will showcase PenFed's Very Important Buyer Program, which is specifically designed to help borrowers reduce closing costs and other expenses associated with purchasing a home.

## The Very Important Buyer Program Explained

The Very Important Buyer Program offers homebuyers strategic advantages when they establish their lending relationship before house hunting. By understanding their financing options and pre-approval status ahead of time, buyers can make more informed decisions about properties within their budget and negotiate from a position of financial clarity.

## Expert Guidance During Open House Weekend

During the October 23-25 event, PenFed representatives will be available to educate attendees on best practices for home purchasing. The open houses will provide prospective buyers with information about mortgage options, pre-approval processes, and ways to minimize expenses throughout the transaction.

## Taking Action Before the Home Search

PenFed's message resonates with a simple but powerful principle: preparation leads to savings. By consulting with a lender before touring homes, buyers gain clarity on their purchasing power, can better understand monthly payment scenarios, and avoid costly mistakes that often occur when offers are made without full financial knowledge.

The National Open House Weekend represents an opportunity for homebuyers across the country to access expert mortgage advice and learn about programs specifically designed to reduce their closing costs. For those considering a home purchase, connecting with PenFed Credit Union during this educational event could lead to thousands in savings when closing day arrives.
