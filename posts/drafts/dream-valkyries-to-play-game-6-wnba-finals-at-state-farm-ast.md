## Game 6 WNBA Finals Matchup Scheduled for Thursday Afternoon

The Atlanta Dream will take on the Las Vegas Valkyries in Game 6 of the WNBA Finals on Thursday at State Farm Arena. The contest is scheduled to tip off at 12:30 p.m. ET, marking a daytime matchup for the crucial playoff game.

## Broadcast Adjustments for Thursday's Schedule

The early start time for the WNBA Finals game has necessitated schedule adjustments for other sporting events. The Cleveland Cavaliers and Atlanta Hawks NBA game, originally scheduled for an earlier time slot, will now begin at 8 p.m. ET to accommodate the afternoon basketball championship contest at the same venue.

The WNBA Finals series between the Dream and Valkyries has progressed to Game 6, indicating a competitive matchup between the two franchises. The matinee scheduling for this pivotal game provides basketball fans with an opportunity to watch championship-level play during afternoon hours.
