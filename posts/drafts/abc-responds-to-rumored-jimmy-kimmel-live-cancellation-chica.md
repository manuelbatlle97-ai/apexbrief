## ABC Sets Record Straight on 'Jimmy Kimmel Live!'

ABC is moving to quash rumors circulating about the future of "Jimmy Kimmel Live!" The network issued a statement addressing widespread speculation that it planned to cancel the long-running late-night talk show at the end of its current season.

## Response to Speculation

The network's clarification comes as industry observers and media outlets have questioned the show's status. ABC's response indicates the network remains committed to the program, which has been a staple of its late-night lineup for nearly two decades.

## 'Jimmy Kimmel Live!' Background

"Jimmy Kimmel Live!" has been on the air since 2003 and has become one of television's most recognizable late-night programs. The show, hosted by Jimmy Kimmel, features celebrity interviews, comedy sketches, and musical performances. It has maintained a significant presence in ABC's programming schedule and continues to attract notable guests and viewers.

## Industry Context

Late-night television has undergone significant changes in recent years, with networks regularly evaluating their programming strategies and viewership metrics. The cancellation rumors may have stemmed from broader discussions about late-night television's evolving landscape and changing viewer habits.

## Looking Ahead

With ABC's denial of the cancellation reports, "Jimmy Kimmel Live!" appears positioned to continue its run. The network's statement effectively counters the speculation that had circulated among media outlets and industry insiders regarding the show's future beyond the current season.
