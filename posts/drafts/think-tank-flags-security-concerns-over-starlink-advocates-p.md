## Think Tank Warns of Starlink Geopolitical Risks

The Kutniti Foundation, a Delhi-based operational think tank, has released a research paper titled "An appraisal of technology for geopolitical control" that raises significant security concerns about the expansion of Starlink in India. The report examines the potential implications of allowing foreign-controlled satellite internet infrastructure to operate within Indian territory and advocates for a more protective stance on the issue.

## Geopolitical Control Through Technology

The research paper focuses on how satellite internet services like Starlink could be leveraged for geopolitical influence and control. The Kutniti Foundation emphasizes that allowing a single foreign entity to maintain such critical infrastructure could pose risks to national sovereignty and security interests. The think tank argues that satellite-based internet systems represent a new frontier in technology-enabled geopolitical competition among nations.

## Call for Protectionist Measures

In response to these concerns, the Kutniti Foundation's research advocates for India to adopt protectionist policies regarding satellite internet services. The foundation suggests that India should carefully evaluate and potentially restrict foreign control over critical communication infrastructure. This recommendation aligns with broader discussions in India about maintaining technological sovereignty and protecting national interests in the space sector.

## Broader Context of Space Technology Debates

The report comes at a time when satellite internet services are becoming increasingly important for global connectivity. Starlink, operated by SpaceX, has been expanding its satellite constellation globally and has shown interest in operating within India. The Kutniti Foundation's analysis suggests that policymakers should consider not just the economic benefits of such services, but also the strategic implications of relying on foreign-controlled infrastructure for critical communications.

## India's Path Forward

As India continues to develop its digital infrastructure and space capabilities, discussions about foreign satellite internet services will likely become more prominent. The Kutniti Foundation's research paper contributes to this conversation by highlighting security and sovereignty considerations that may influence India's regulatory approach to companies like Starlink.
