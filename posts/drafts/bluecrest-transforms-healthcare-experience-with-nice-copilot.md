## UK Health Company Achieves Major Efficiency Gains with AI Technology

Bluecrest, a leading UK health intelligence company, has successfully transformed its contact center operations using NiCE's CXone platform and NiCE Copilot technology. The implementation has delivered significant improvements in operational efficiency, with the company reporting a 94% improvement in call answer rates.

## Modernizing Healthcare Customer Support

The deployment of NiCE's AI-powered solutions marks a major shift in how Bluecrest manages customer interactions. By integrating the CXone platform with NiCE Copilot, the company has created a more responsive and insight-driven contact center environment. The technology enables faster call handling and improved customer service delivery across Bluecrest's operations.

## Real-World Results in Healthcare Operations

The 94% improvement in call answer rates represents a substantial enhancement to Bluecrest's service capabilities. This metric reflects the platform's ability to streamline call routing, reduce wait times, and optimize agent productivity. For a health intelligence organization, improved call handling directly translates to better patient support and faster access to critical health information.

## AI-Powered Insights Drive Operational Excellence

The NiCE CXone platform provides Bluecrest with advanced analytics and AI-driven insights that enhance decision-making across the contact center. The NiCE Copilot assists agents in real-time, offering recommendations and automating routine tasks. This intelligent approach allows human agents to focus on complex healthcare inquiries while the AI handles repetitive processes.

## Industry Impact and Future Outlook

Bluecrest's successful implementation demonstrates the growing importance of AI technology in healthcare support operations. As healthcare organizations face increasing pressure to improve service quality while managing costs, AI-powered contact center solutions offer a compelling solution. The partnership between Bluecrest and NiCE showcases how established health intelligence companies are embracing digital transformation to serve their customers better.

The announcement comes as healthcare organizations continue to invest in modernizing their customer-facing operations, recognizing that efficient communication channels are essential to delivering quality patient care.
