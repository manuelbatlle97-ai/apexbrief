## Hayes Brings Experience to USMNT Coaching Search

U.S. Soccer has appointed Emma Hayes, head coach of the United States Women's National Team, to serve on the committee responsible for identifying the next head coach of the men's national team. The decision to include Hayes in the hiring process reflects the federation's commitment to leveraging experienced voices from across the organization as it seeks a successor to Mauricio Pochettino.

## Why This Move Makes Strategic Sense

Hayes brings considerable credibility and institutional knowledge to the committee. As the USWNT head coach, she has successfully navigated the pressures of leading a major U.S. national team, managed player expectations, and delivered results on the international stage. Her perspective on what it takes to build a winning program and handle the unique demands of coaching for the USMNT offers valuable insight that extends beyond the men's program.

Including a women's coach in the men's hiring process also demonstrates U.S. Soccer's intent to break down silos between the two national teams and foster greater collaboration across the organization. Hayes' success with the women's team provides a proven track record that lends credibility to her input on what qualifies as an elite coaching candidate.

## Strengthening the Search Process

The committee's composition will likely influence the type of candidate pursued for the USMNT position. Hayes' involvement suggests the federation is looking for candidates who can build comprehensive player development systems, manage high expectations, and deliver consistent international results. Her presence on the committee could help ensure the next hire aligns with broader organizational goals.

## Moving Forward

Finding Pochettino's successor represents a critical juncture for U.S. Soccer as it looks to stabilize and improve the men's national team program. With Hayes contributing to the decision-making process, the federation is signaling that the search will benefit from diverse perspectives and proven leadership experience from within its own ranks.

The hiring committee's work will be closely watched by fans and stakeholders eager to see who will lead the USMNT in its next phase of development.
