## Crowd Surge Causes Multiple Injuries at Durga Puja Pandal

A crowd surge at the Chorbagan Durga Puja pandal in Kolkata has resulted in at least 25 people sustaining injuries, according to reports from BusinessLine. The incident occurred when rainfall triggered a sudden rush among the large gathering of devotees visiting the popular pandal during the festival celebrations.

The exact circumstances leading to the surge remain under investigation by local authorities. Eyewitnesses reported that as rain began to fall, a mass movement toward shelter areas created a dangerous crush among the densely packed crowd. The pandal, one of Kolkata's prominent Durga Puja venues, had attracted a significant number of visitors, increasing the vulnerability to such incidents.

## Emergency Response and Medical Treatment

First responders quickly mobilized to the scene following the incident. The injured individuals were provided immediate medical assistance, with authorities documenting their conditions and injuries. While the exact nature and severity of injuries have not been fully detailed, initial reports suggest a range of impact-related trauma sustained during the surge.

## Unconfirmed Death Reports

While social media and local reports initially circulated claims of fatalities, death reports remain unconfirmed at this time. Authorities have not issued official statements confirming any deaths connected to the incident. The continued verification of casualty numbers underscores the need for accurate information during emergencies of this scale.

## Safety Concerns at Major Events

The incident raises renewed questions about crowd management and safety protocols at major festival gatherings in India. Durga Puja celebrations, among the most significant cultural events in Kolkata, regularly draw hundreds of thousands of visitors across multiple pandals. The challenge of managing such large crowds during unpredictable weather conditions remains a persistent concern for event organizers and authorities.

The Kolkata Municipal Corporation and event management officials have not yet released comprehensive details about preventive measures that were in place at the Chorbagan pandal or whether additional safety protocols will be implemented for remaining festival days.
