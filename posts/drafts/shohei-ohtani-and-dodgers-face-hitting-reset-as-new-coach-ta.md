## Dodgers Address Offensive Inconsistency

The Los Angeles Dodgers are making a significant coaching move to address a costly weakness that plagued their otherwise powerful 2026 offense. Despite ranking among Major League Baseball's elite offenses during the regular season, the team repeatedly failed to capitalize on scoring opportunities when they mattered most—a pattern that became painfully evident during postseason play.

## Pedro Guerrero Joins Coaching Staff

The Dodgers have brought in Pedro Guerrero to serve as a hitting coach, tasking him with diagnosing and correcting the team's inconsistent approach at the plate. Guerrero's appointment reflects the organization's recognition that raw talent alone was insufficient to convert their offensive potential into tangible results. His hiring suggests the front office believes mechanical and strategic adjustments could unlock the team's full capability.

## Regular Season Strength, Postseason Weakness

The contrast between the Dodgers' regular-season performance and their postseason execution revealed a troubling gap. While the team generated plenty of scoring chances throughout 2026, their inability to execute in high-leverage situations cost them dearly when facing elimination games and advancing through the playoffs. This pattern suggests issues beyond individual talent—pointing instead to approach, discipline, and consistency in crucial moments.

## Focus on Plate Discipline and Mechanics

Guerrero's mandate centers on refining how Dodgers hitters approach their at-bats. With Shohei Ohtani and other talented offensive players on the roster, the challenge lies not in finding talent but in optimizing how that talent is deployed. The new hitting coach will likely focus on pitch selection, swing mechanics, and situational awareness to help the team convert opportunities into runs.

## Looking Ahead to 2027

The Dodgers' front office is banking on coaching adjustments as a path to postseason success. By addressing the gap between regular-season dominance and playoff execution, they hope to transform their 2026 shortcomings into 2027 strength. Whether Guerrero can unlock the adjustments needed remains to be seen, but the organization's willingness to make coaching changes demonstrates their commitment to maximizing their roster's potential.
