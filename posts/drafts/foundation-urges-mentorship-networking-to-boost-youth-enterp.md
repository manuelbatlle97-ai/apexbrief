## Foundation Pushes for Youth Entrepreneurship Support

Twani Foundation has launched a call for increased mentorship and networking opportunities aimed at empowering young Nigerians to establish and grow their own businesses. The foundation's initiative recognizes mentorship and strategic business connections as critical factors in fostering a thriving entrepreneurial ecosystem that can contribute meaningfully to the nation's economic development.

## The Role of Mentorship in Business Success

The foundation emphasizes that mentorship serves as a vital bridge between aspiring entrepreneurs and experienced business leaders. Through structured guidance and knowledge transfer, young business owners gain access to invaluable insights, industry expertise, and practical advice that can help them navigate the complexities of starting and scaling ventures. This mentoring relationship provides emerging entrepreneurs with real-world perspectives on business challenges, market dynamics, and growth strategies that would otherwise take years to acquire independently.

## Networking as a Catalyst for Growth

Beyond mentorship, Twani Foundation highlights the importance of robust business networks in creating opportunities for young entrepreneurs. Networking enables emerging business owners to forge connections with potential investors, collaborators, suppliers, and customers. These relationships form the foundation for partnerships, funding opportunities, and market access that are essential for business expansion and sustainability.

## Economic Impact and Long-Term Vision

The foundation's advocacy underscores the broader economic implications of supporting youth enterprise. By cultivating a generation of confident, well-guided entrepreneurs, Nigeria can unlock significant job creation potential, drive innovation, and stimulate economic growth across various sectors. Young entrepreneurs who receive proper mentorship and networking support are better positioned to build resilient businesses that contribute to their communities and the national economy.

## Call for Action

Twani Foundation's appeal represents a push for stakeholders—including government agencies, established businesses, educational institutions, and civil society organizations—to collaborate in building comprehensive support systems for young entrepreneurs. The foundation advocates for programs that connect experienced mentors with promising young business founders and create networking platforms where ideas, resources, and opportunities can be freely exchanged.

As Nigeria continues to face economic challenges, strengthening support systems for youth entrepreneurship remains crucial for sustainable development and poverty reduction.
