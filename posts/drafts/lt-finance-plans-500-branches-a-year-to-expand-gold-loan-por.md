## L&T Finance Expands Gold Loan Strategy

L&T Finance is plotting an ambitious expansion of its gold loan business, planning to open 500 new branches annually as part of its long-term growth strategy. The financial services company is targeting significant growth in this segment, with gold loans expected to exceed 10 percent of its total loan book by 2031.

## Portfolio Restructuring Goals

The expansion aligns with L&T Finance's broader portfolio restructuring plans. The company aims to shift its loan portfolio mix toward a 60:40 ratio favoring secured lending. This strategic shift reflects growing demand for gold-backed loans, which typically carry lower default rates and provide better risk management for lenders.

## Aggressive Branch Network Growth

With plans to establish 500 branches yearly, L&T Finance is making a significant capital commitment to reach underserved markets and existing customers seeking gold loan products. This branch expansion strategy demonstrates the company's confidence in the gold loan segment's growth potential and its ability to serve diverse customer bases across India.

## Market Opportunity

Gold loans have emerged as an attractive product category for both lenders and borrowers in India. For lenders, gold-backed loans offer security through tangible collateral, reducing credit risk. For borrowers, these loans provide quick access to funds without extensive documentation requirements, making them particularly appealing in both urban and rural markets.

## Long-Term Vision

L&T Finance's strategic focus on gold loans by 2031 reflects its confidence in the segment's sustained growth trajectory. By restructuring its portfolio toward secured lending and expanding its branch network substantially, the company is positioning itself to capture significant market share in the gold loan space while maintaining strong credit quality standards.
