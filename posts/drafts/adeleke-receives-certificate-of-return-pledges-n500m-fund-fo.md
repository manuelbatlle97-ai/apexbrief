## Adeleke Receives Certificate, Commits to Victim Support

Osun State Governor Ademola Adeleke received his Certificate of Return from the Independent National Electoral Commission (INEC) on Wednesday, officially confirming his election victory. During the ceremony in Osogbo, the governor made significant commitments to address the impact of electoral violence experienced during the election period.

## N500 Million Fund Announced

Adeleke announced the establishment of a N500 million fund dedicated to supporting victims of electoral violence. The initiative represents the governor's commitment to healing and reconciliation following the contentious election process. The fund aims to provide financial assistance to individuals and families affected by violence-related incidents during the electoral period.

## Educational Support for Affected Children

Beyond the direct financial relief, Adeleke pledged to provide scholarships for children of electoral violence victims. This educational component seeks to ensure that young people affected by the violence can continue their studies without financial barriers. The scholarship program reflects a broader commitment to supporting the next generation in Osun State.

## Recognition and Moving Forward

The receipt of the Certificate of Return marked the formal confirmation of Adeleke's electoral victory and his mandate to lead Osun State. The governor's immediate focus on victim support demonstrates his prioritization of social cohesion and healing as he takes office.

These initiatives signal Adeleke's intention to address grievances and foster unity across the state following what has been described as a contentious election period. The combined approach of direct financial assistance through the N500 million fund and educational support through scholarships indicates a multifaceted strategy to support affected communities.

The announcements come as Osun State moves forward under new leadership, with the governor setting an early tone of reconciliation and social responsibility in his administration's approach to governance.
