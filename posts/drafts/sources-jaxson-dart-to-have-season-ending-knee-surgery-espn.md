Dart's absence creates an immediate need for the Giants to adjust their quarterback rotation. The team will need to rely on backup options to fill the void at the position during the remainder of the season. Season-ending knee surgeries typically require an extended recovery period that extends into the offseason. The Giants will work with their medical staff to ensure proper rehabilitation and recovery protocols.
