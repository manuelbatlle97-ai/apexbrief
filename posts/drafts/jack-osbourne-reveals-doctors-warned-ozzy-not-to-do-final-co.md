Jack Osbourne is shedding light on the health struggles his legendary father Ozzy Osbourne faced before his passing. During an appearance on the podcast "Great Company with Jamie Laing," Jack revealed that doctors had explicitly warned Ozzy against performing at his farewell show, expressing concerns about the rocker's physical condition at the time.

## Medical Advice Unheeded

Despite the medical professionals' cautionary guidance, Ozzy proceeded with his final concert performance.
