## Crocs Launches Weekend Flash Sale with Stacked Discounts

Crocs is running a limited-time weekend promotion featuring an extra 50% off on a selection of their most popular styles. The sale applies to over 260 items currently marked down, with the additional discount automatically applied when you add items to your cart at checkout.

## How the Sale Works

The promotion is straightforward: browse Crocs' sale section, add your chosen items to your cart, and the extra 50% discount will be calculated at checkout. It's important to note that prices won't reflect the full discount until items are physically in your cart. The offer applies exclusively to sale variations only, meaning some colors or styles may not be eligible depending on their current sale status. Shoppers should verify that their preferred items are showing the discounted price before completing their purchase.

## Free Shipping Offer

As part of the weekend promotion, Crocs is also offering free shipping on orders over $49.99. This benefit makes it an ideal time for customers to stock up on multiple pairs or gift items. Combined with the stacked discounts, the free shipping threshold is easily achievable for most purchases.

## Limited-Time Availability

As a weekend sale, this promotion is expected to have a limited window. Customers interested in taking advantage of the deal should act soon, as inventory on popular styles and sizes may deplete quickly. Past Crocs sales have attracted significant traffic, so availability could be restricted as the weekend progresses.
