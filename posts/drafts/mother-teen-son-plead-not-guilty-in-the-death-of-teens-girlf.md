## Mother and Son Enter Not Guilty Pleas in College Student's Death

Royce Moser and his mother Rachel Moser appeared in court on Tuesday for their arraignments in connection with the death of Karoline Heintz, a college student whose body was discovered in her car in August. Both defendants entered not guilty pleas during the proceedings, maintaining their innocence in the case that has drawn significant public attention.

## Details of the August Discovery

Karoline Heintz was found dead in her vehicle in August, prompting a criminal investigation that ultimately led to charges against the Moser family members. The circumstances surrounding her death sparked widespread concern in the community and launched an extensive investigation by law enforcement authorities.

## Legal Proceedings Underway

The Tuesday arraignments marked a significant development in the case, with both Royce and Rachel Moser appearing before the court. Arraignments allow defendants to hear charges against them and enter pleas. The not guilty pleas entered by both defendants indicate they will contest the charges through the legal system.

## Investigation and Charges

The relationship between the Mosers and the victim appears to be a central focus of the investigation. Royce Moser's connection to Karoline Heintz as her teen boyfriend established a critical link that prompted authorities to pursue charges against both him and his mother. The nature of the charges and evidence presented by prosecutors remain key elements as the case progresses through the court system.

## Next Steps

With both defendants having entered not guilty pleas, the case will now proceed through the judicial process. Further court dates and preliminary hearings are expected as the legal proceedings continue. The case highlights the serious nature of homicide investigations and the judicial system's role in determining guilt or innocence based on presented evidence and legal arguments from both prosecution and defense teams.
