## Botafogo Celebrates Supporters' Week with Municipal Leadership

Botafogo opened its doors on Tuesday to welcome Leandro Guimarães, the Secretary of Sports and Leisure for Duque de Caxias, as part of the official Duque-Caxiense Supporters' Week. The event underscores the growing connection between the football club and local municipal government in recognizing the importance of fan communities.

## Official Municipal Recognition

The Duque-Caxiense Supporters' Week represents an official initiative of the municipality of Duque de Caxias, bringing together stakeholders from both the sports and civic sectors. The visit by Secretary Guimarães demonstrates the municipality's commitment to celebrating and supporting the region's football culture and dedicated fan base.

## Strengthening Fan Engagement

Botafogo's participation in hosting this supporters' week reflects the club's ongoing efforts to engage with its community and supporters across various municipalities. Such events provide opportunities to strengthen relationships between clubs, fans, and local government bodies, fostering a collaborative approach to sports development and community engagement.
