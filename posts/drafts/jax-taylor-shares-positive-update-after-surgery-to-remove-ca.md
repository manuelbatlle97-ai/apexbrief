## Reality TV Star Jax Taylor Updates Fans on Cancer Surgery Recovery

Jax Taylor is maintaining a positive outlook following surgery to remove a cancerous tumor. The 47-year-old reality television personality shared an update with his followers on Instagram Friday, November 20, expressing gratitude for his medical team's care and expertise.

## Praising His Surgical Team

In his Instagram post, Taylor shared a photo alongside his surgeon while thanking the medical professionals involved in his procedure. "This is my incredible surgeon, @heydoctormike, who performed my surgery," he wrote, highlighting the surgeon's role in his treatment. The Valley alum's message reflected his appreciation for the care he received during this challenging time.

## Waiting for Results

While Taylor has shared his initial recovery, he is currently awaiting the results of his surgery as the next step in his medical journey. The former Bravo star has chosen to keep his supporters informed during his health battle, using his social media platform to share updates and express gratitude for the support he has received.
