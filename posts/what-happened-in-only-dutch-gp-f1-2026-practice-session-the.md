## Antonelli Takes Early Advantage at Zandvoort

Kimi Antonelli claimed the top spot in the only practice session for the 2026 Dutch Grand Prix, signaling strong form as Formula 1's mid-season push begins. The Mercedes driver demonstrated commanding pace on the tight Zandvoort circuit, edging out McLaren's Lando Norris to set the session's benchmark time.

## McLaren Remains Competitive

Norris's second-place finish underscores McLaren's continued competitiveness in the 2026 campaign. The British driver and his team have been among the frontrunners throughout the season and showed no signs of slowing down during the Dutch GP preparations. The narrow gap between Antonelli and Norris suggested competitive racing conditions ahead for the weekend.

## Second Half Kickoff

The practice session served as the opening action for F1's second half of the 2026 season. Teams used the single practice opportunity to gather data on car performance, tire behavior, and track conditions at the demanding Zandvoort venue. The circuit's unique characteristics—featuring high-speed corners and limited overtaking opportunities—made qualifying performance particularly crucial for drivers preparing their race strategies.

## Looking Ahead to the Weekend

Antonelli's practice dominance provides Mercedes momentum heading into qualifying and race day. The performance suggests the German manufacturer has maintained its competitive edge through the season's midpoint. Norris and McLaren will look to close the gap during qualifying to ensure a strong starting position for Sunday's Grand Prix.

The Dutch GP represents a key moment in the 2026 F1 season as the grid prepares for the season's crucial second half. Teams will continue analyzing the data gathered during practice as they fine-tune setups for qualifying and the race itself. The proximity between Antonelli and Norris hints at competitive battles to come at one of Europe's most demanding circuits.
