## Affordable Nonstop Service Between DC and Toronto

Travelers looking to visit Canada this summer can take advantage of significantly discounted round-trip airfares between Washington DC and Toronto. Air Canada and Porter Airlines are offering nonstop flights from DC-area airports to Billy Bishop Toronto City Airport for $213 to $216 per person, according to deals posted on travel deal aggregator Slickdeals.

## Flight Details and Availability

The economy basic fares apply to nonstop service departing from either Washington Dulles International Airport (IAD) or Ronald Reagan Washington National Airport (DCA) to Toronto's Billy Bishop City Airport (YTZ). These rates are available for travel during the August through October 2026 window, providing flexibility for summer and early fall trips. The fares were confirmed to be available through Expedia in addition to directly through the airlines.

## What to Know About Economy Basic Fares

Passengers should be aware that these economy basic fares typically come with limitations compared to standard economy tickets. Economy basic usually includes a carry-on bag and personal item, but may not include seat selection, checked baggage, or other amenities. Travelers should verify baggage policies and any additional fees before booking, as restrictions vary by airline.

## Planning Your Trip

For those considering a cross-border vacation, the pricing represents a substantial savings compared to typical transatlantic and cross-border airfares. The nonstop flights eliminate the need for connections, providing a direct route between the two cities. Travelers interested in these fares should book through Expedia or directly with Air Canada or Porter Airlines, as promotional pricing is often limited and subject to availability.

## Comparison Value

Round-trip nonstop fares in the $200-$220 range offer competitive pricing for the DC-to-Toronto route. Savvy travelers monitoring deal sites like Slickdeals can identify similar opportunities for other travel dates and routes, potentially unlocking significant savings on summer and fall travel plans.
