## Tragic Incident in Sundargarh District

Three young girls have drowned while bathing in the Sankh river in Odisha's Sundargarh district. The minors, aged between 6 and 9 years old, were bathing in the river under Raiboga police jurisdiction when the tragic incident occurred. The discovery of the drowning has sent shockwaves through the local community and raised serious concerns about water safety in the region.

## Police Response and Investigation

Local police responded to the incident and registered an unnatural death case following standard procedures. Authorities launched an investigation into the circumstances surrounding the drowning. The case has been filed under appropriate sections to determine the exact events that led to the deaths of the three children. Officials are examining factors that may have contributed to the tragedy, including water conditions and supervision at the bathing site.

## Community Impact

The incident highlights the ongoing risks associated with river bathing, particularly for children in rural areas. The Sankh river, a natural attraction for local residents, has become the site of this devastating loss. Such tragedies underscore the importance of implementing water safety measures and educating communities about the dangers of unsupervised bathing in natural water bodies.

## Call for Safety Measures

The drowning of three young girls serves as a grim reminder of the need for enhanced safety protocols around natural water sources. Authorities and community leaders are expected to review safety measures at popular bathing sites. Parents and guardians are being urged to exercise caution and maintain close supervision when children are near water bodies. Public awareness campaigns about water safety and the risks of drowning in rivers remain critical, especially in areas with limited swimming and rescue infrastructure.

The loss of these young lives has deeply affected the local community, prompting renewed discussions about preventive measures and child safety in the region.
