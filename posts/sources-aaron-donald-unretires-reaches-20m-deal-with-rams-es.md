## Donald Returns to Rams Fold

All-Pro defensive tackle Aaron Donald is unretiring to play for the Los Angeles Rams after stepping away from professional football. According to ESPN's Adam Schefter and NFL Network's Ian Rapoport, Donald agreed to a one-year contract worth $20 million, reuniting him with the franchise where he has spent his entire NFL career.

Donald's decision to return comes as the Rams continue their quest for another Super Bowl title. The defensive star had previously announced his retirement, but the opportunity to contribute to another championship run persuaded him to return to the field. His addition strengthens the Rams' defensive line as they prepare for the upcoming season.

## Championship Aspirations

The timing of Donald's comeback reflects the Rams' competitive window and their commitment to assembling a championship-caliber roster. The franchise has already invested significantly in other key players, and bringing back one of the league's premier defensive talents demonstrates their determination to compete at the highest level. Donald's presence on the defensive line provides the Rams with one of the most dominant forces in professional football.

Donald has been a cornerstone of the Rams organization throughout his tenure, consistently delivering elite performance and establishing himself as one of the greatest defensive linemen in NFL history. His return represents a significant boost to the team's defensive capabilities as they prepare for the regular season.

The one-year agreement worth $20 million reflects Donald's star status and the value he brings to the franchise.
