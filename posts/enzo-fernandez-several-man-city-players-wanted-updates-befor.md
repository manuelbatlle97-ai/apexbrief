## Man City Players Sought Fernandez Updates

Manchester City manager Enzo Maresca has disclosed that multiple players at the club were keen to receive information about Enzo Fernandez's transfer status during the final stretch of the transfer window. The interest from within the squad highlighted the significance of the Argentine midfielder's potential arrival at the Etihad Stadium.

Fernandez ultimately completed his move to Manchester City as the transfer deadline loomed, joining the Premier League champions in what represents a major signing for the club. Maresca's comments shed light on how the transfer dominated conversations within the squad during the hectic final days of the window.

## Squad Interest in the Transfer

The manager's revelation suggests that Fernandez's impending arrival generated considerable excitement among Manchester City's playing staff. Players wanting regular updates on such a high-profile signing is not uncommon, particularly when it involves a midfielder expected to contribute to the team's ambitions for the season.

Maresca's willingness to discuss the squad's interest in the transfer offers insight into the internal dynamics at Manchester City during the transfer period. The level of engagement from existing players demonstrates the profile and potential impact of the new recruit within the changing room.

## Completion of the Deal

Fernandez's signing represents an important addition to Manchester City's midfield options as the club continues its pursuit of domestic and European success. The completion of the transfer, despite coming late in the window, showcases the club's determination to strengthen their squad during the final opportunity of the summer market.
