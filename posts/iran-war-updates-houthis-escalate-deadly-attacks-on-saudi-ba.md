## Houthis Launch Fresh Attacks in Yemen

Yemen's Houthi rebels have escalated military operations against Saudi-backed government troops, according to reports from Yemeni state media. The fresh attacks underscore the persistent violence plaguing the war-torn nation, where the Iran-aligned Houthis have maintained a significant military presence despite years of Saudi-led coalition airstrikes.

The latest assault represents a continuation of the Houthis' strategy to challenge the Saudi-backed government's control of key territories. The rebel group has repeatedly demonstrated its capacity to conduct coordinated military operations despite international efforts to contain the conflict. State media reports indicate the attacks targeted government positions, though specific casualty figures were not immediately disclosed.

## Cruz Calls for Regime Change Policy

Meanwhile, Senator Ted Cruz has urged the incoming Trump administration to adopt a more aggressive stance toward Iran's government. Cruz specifically called for the United States to arm Iranian protesters, effectively supporting efforts aimed at what he described as "regime collapse." The Texas Republican's comments reflect growing calls among some conservative lawmakers for a harder line on Iran policy.

Cruz's statement suggests that elements within the GOP view arming Iranian opposition groups as a viable strategy to challenge Tehran's authority. This approach represents a potential shift from previous administration policies and signals possible changes to how the U.S. may engage with Iran-related issues moving forward.

## Regional Implications

The Houthis' escalation in Yemen and the political debate over Iran policy highlight the interconnected nature of Middle Eastern conflicts. The Houthi movement has maintained close ties to Iran, receiving military support and training, making Yemen's conflict directly relevant to broader U.S.-Iran relations.

The developments come as the Trump administration prepares to take office, with foreign policy toward Iran and the Middle East likely to be reassessed. Cruz's comments suggest that some influential Republicans will push for policies aimed at undermining the Iranian government and supporting opposition movements. The combination of renewed Houthi attacks and calls for regime change strategy indicates the region faces potential for increased tensions and escalating conflict in the coming months.
