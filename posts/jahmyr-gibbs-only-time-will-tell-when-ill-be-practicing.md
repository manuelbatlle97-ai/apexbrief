When asked about his return to practice, Gibbs offered a noncommittal response, indicating that only time will tell when he resumes his place in the Lions' lineup. The lack of a definitive timeline suggests the contract discussions remain in active negotiations, with both sides working toward an agreement.

## Extension Discussions Ongoing

The Lions and Gibbs are in the process of discussing a contract extension that would affect the running back's future with the franchise. Contract extensions are common in professional football, allowing teams and players to realign their agreements to reflect market value and player performance.

## Impact on Training Camp

Gibbs' absence from training camp practices represents a notable situation for the Lions as they prepare for the upcoming season. Running backs play a crucial role in any NFL offense, and their participation in training camp helps establish continuity with quarterbacks and develop timing on plays.

## Looking Ahead

As negotiations progress, both the Lions organization and Gibbs will need to reach a resolution that satisfies both parties.
