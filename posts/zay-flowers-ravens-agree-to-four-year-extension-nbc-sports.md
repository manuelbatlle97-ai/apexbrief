## Ravens Lock Up Zay Flowers with Major Contract Extension

Wide receiver Zay Flowers and the Baltimore Ravens have agreed to a four-year contract extension, securing the talented pass-catcher as a cornerstone of the franchise's offense. According to reports, the deal is worth $140 million with $108 million guaranteed, reflecting the team's confidence in the receiver's ability to lead their passing game for years to come.

## A Growing Star in Baltimore

Flowers has emerged as an important target for the Ravens offense since entering the league. The extension signals the team's commitment to building around him as they look to compete in a competitive AFC North. By locking him in long-term, Baltimore ensures continuity at a key position and demonstrates its faith in the receiver's trajectory and impact on the field.

## Financial Commitment

The guaranteed money in the deal underscores the organization's serious investment in Flowers' future with the team. With $108 million guaranteed out of the $140 million total value, the receiver has secured substantial financial protection, which is typical for top-tier receivers in today's NFL market.

## Building for the Future

This extension comes as the Ravens continue to build their roster around quarterback Lamar Jackson and a strong defensive unit. Securing Flowers long-term helps establish stability at the receiver position and allows the organization to plan its salary cap commitments accordingly as it pursues playoff success.

The deal positions the Ravens to maintain offensive firepower while managing their financial obligations across the roster. With Flowers locked in, Baltimore can focus on complementing his talents with additional supporting pieces as they work toward achieving their championship aspirations in the coming seasons.
