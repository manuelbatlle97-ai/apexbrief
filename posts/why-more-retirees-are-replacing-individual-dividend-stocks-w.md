## The Risk of Individual Dividend Stocks

Dividend-paying stocks have long been a cornerstone of retirement portfolios, offering a steady income stream to retirees. However, investors are discovering that even companies with impressive dividend histories can abruptly cut payouts. A company may demonstrate a track record of raising dividends for 20 consecutive years, only to slash its dividend overnight—a move that devastates both the income stream and stock price for shareholders who depend on that revenue.

This unpredictability has prompted a fundamental shift in how retirees approach income investing. The risk of sudden dividend cuts represents a critical vulnerability for those living on fixed income, as such cuts can significantly impact their financial security and retirement plans.

## The Appeal of Income ETFs

Low-cost income exchange-traded funds (ETFs) offer a compelling alternative by providing built-in diversification across multiple dividend-paying companies. Instead of relying on the dividend policies of individual corporations, retirees can spread their investment across numerous holdings, reducing the impact of any single company's dividend cut.

Income ETFs typically charge lower expense ratios than actively managed funds and provide broader exposure to dividend-paying sectors. This diversification approach allows retirees to maintain consistent income while reducing the volatility associated with individual stock selection and company-specific risks.

## Growing Investor Adoption

The migration toward income ETFs reflects a broader trend among retirement investors seeking more predictable and stable income sources. Financial advisors increasingly recommend this strategy as a way to balance income generation with risk management, particularly for those in their withdrawal years.

## Key Considerations

While no investment strategy is completely risk-free, income ETFs provide a layer of protection through diversification. Retirees considering this transition should evaluate their current portfolio composition and consider consulting with financial advisors to determine the best approach for their individual circumstances.

The shift from individual dividend stocks to income ETFs demonstrates how retirement investors are adapting their strategies to protect their financial security in an unpredictable market environment.
